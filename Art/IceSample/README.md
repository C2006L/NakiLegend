# Ice harbor sample art

The runtime resources are `Assets/NakiPrototype/Resources/NakiArt/IceSample/atlas.png` and `floor.png`. Generated with the imagegen skill; original generated files were copied intact. Runtime sprite regions were authored against the actual silhouettes rather than a rigid grid, so the wider beast does not lose its horns or include neighboring art. No raster editing or external art copying was used.

## Atlas prompt

Production 2D top down fantasy dungeon polished pixel art sprite atlas, transparent PNG, exactly 4 columns x 2 rows uniform cells with generous blank gutters, no text grid backgrounds shadows or cropping. Consistent three-quarter overhead view upper-left lighting, crisp dark outlines and icy blue ivory slate brown palette. Row1: cell1 small ice-armored sentry with crystalline shield and short ice axe; cell2 floating three-pronged crystalline ice wisp ranged enemy; cell3 large quadruped frost armored beast boss, armadillo boar silhouette with huge layered translucent ice plates and two heavy horns, four clear feet; cell4 elderly lighthouse keeper NPC in thick ivory fur coat with a small warm amber lantern. Row2: cell1 low solid ice boulder with embedded rune crystal; cell2 opened weathered expedition logbook on a short icy wooden stand; cell3 small snow-covered moored wooden rowboat with tiny pier segment, no water background; cell4 upright blue signal crystal on a low stone pedestal, magical carved marks without lettering. Each subject centered fully inside own equal cell, no overlap between cells, no ground plane, matching a small blue-cloaked chibi adventurer in an action RPG. Front-right view for enemies. Do not include green leaves plants wells desert props.

## Floor prompt

Seamless square top-down ground texture for polished pixel art 2D frozen harbor dungeon RPG. Flat orthographic stone paving of broad muted blue grey slate blocks with thin frosted seams, lightly windblown ivory snow collected in small irregular patches. Subtle detail, readable empty walkable space, subdued mid value background to contrast bright cyan monsters. Repeating seamless edges. No objects no buildings no walls no characters no text no decorative symbols no perspective horizon no thick outer frame. No grass no wood forest floor. Consistent lighting.

## Scope

This is a first playable sample, with single-pose monsters, procedural facing/bobbing and explicit combat warnings. The signal crystal is a future prop. Full enemy frame sets, bespoke walls/doors, harbor ocean backdrop and complete-island CG remain future work.
