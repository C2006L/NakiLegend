# Ice harbor expansion art

Generated using the **built-in imagegen tool**, transparent background. Copied intact into the repository; the prior atlas was retained. Runtime regions were authored for each actual subject, with independent pivots and obstacle footprints. No raster editing was used.

## Final prompt

Create a NEW production companion sprite atlas for a polished pixel-art top-down 2D fantasy ice harbor RPG. Transparent alpha background. Exactly EIGHT separate subjects in FOUR EQUAL columns and TWO EQUAL rows; generous empty gutters and margins, no overlaps, every subject fully contained in its own cell. No letters, text, grid or backdrop. Coherent crisp dark outlines, moderately detailed chibi RPG pixel shading, muted blue-grey ice, ivory snow and warm dark wood, upper-left illumination, 3/4 overhead camera. Row 1 left to right: (1) compact white frost wolf enemy with dark blue paws, icy fangs and ruffled fur, low quadruped silhouette facing right; (2) short hooded human winter archer enemy in slate blue leather and fur, clearly holding a bent wooden bow properly, facing right; (3) short stone ice-rune acolyte enemy with broad slate hood and glowing cyan rune staff, no human face, facing right; (4) low sturdy snow-covered wooden cargo crate, square footprint, heavy rope lashings. Row 2 left to right: (1) one squat dark wooden barrel capped with snow and iron hoops, distinct from crate; (2) tall narrow blue ice spire rooted in grey rock, vertical blocking silhouette; (3) long low narrow fractured dark ice fissure, sharp irregular blue-white frosted edges, no surrounding ground tile, horizontal orientation; (4) small low pile of broken wooden ship planks lashed together with rope and lightly snow covered. All noncharacter props viewed from identical overhead angle. No green foliage, no desert columns, no puddles, no contact shadows. Enough separation to slice the atlas cleanly. Keep each subject under 75 percent of its cell width and height.
